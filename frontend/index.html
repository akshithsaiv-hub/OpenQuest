🏛️ Official RBI & NABARD KCC Norms

Statutory guidelines protecting farmers from predatory processing fees.

1
Zero Processing Fees: RBI mandates zero processing, inspection, and ledger charges on KCC limits up to ₹3,00,000.
2
No Land Mortgage up to ₹1.60 Lakh: Banks are strictly prohibited from demanding land deed mortgage or charge creation for loans up to ₹1.60 Lakh.
3
Tenant Cultivators & Sharecroppers Eligible: Tenant farmers can secure KCC via CCRC or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) without land ownership.

Mandatory 14-Day Bank SLA: Banks must sanction and disburse complete KCC applications within 14 days of receipt.
